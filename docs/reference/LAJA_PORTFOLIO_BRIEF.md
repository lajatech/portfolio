**Client:** Sheperd Trust MFB (Neo Cash brand)  
**Industry:** FinTech  
**Timeline:** September 2025, 4 months

**The Problem:**  
NeoCash is a business loan solution for MSMEs (Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises) in Nigeria. Their existing platform was:
- Visually outdated — no modern identity
- Required too much manual work from users and staff
- Appeared static with no personality
- Had a broken loan application flow that most users abandoned

**Core design challenge:**  
"How can we reframe the digital loan platform with modern UX?"

- Designed **micro-interactions and fluid transitions** to reduce perceived friction
- Wrote **UX copy** — conversational, warm, and clear — throughout the flow
- Built the old vs. new design comparison as part of the deliverable
- Key screens include: Schedule Visitation, Income Source Addition, Splash Screens, Conversational UI screens
- Delivered a scalable loan application system embedded in a redesigned overall UI

**Results:**
- **+1050%** increase in loan application completion
- **2.5x** feature adoption
- Client testimonial: *"Working with Laja was seamless and inspiring. His work elevated our brand. Gave him a tip and will definitely come back for more projects"* — Blessing Edet, Manager, Neo Cash

**Story arc for case study page:**

## 6. Brand Assets

### Logo
**File:** `La_ja_.svg`  
**Format:** Pure SVG, white fill, viewBox="0 0 419 173"  
**Usage:** Logo is white — ensure it's placed on dark or colored backgrounds where it reads clearly. On white backgrounds, use a dark version (invert to `fill="black"` or use a dark container).  
**The wordmark:** "Làjà" — the brand name. The SVG renders the custom lettering for this.

### Typography
**Display / Heading font:** Special Gothic Expanded One  
**File:** `SpecialGothicExpandedOne-Regular.ttf`  
**Usage:** All hero headings, section titles, large statement text, metric callouts. This is the brand-defining typeface — use it boldly and sparingly.  
**Body / UI font:** System sans-serif stack (e.g. `Inter`, `DM Sans`, or similar clean geometric sans) — to be decided during build. Must pair cleanly with Special Gothic Expanded One.  
**Font loading:** Load `SpecialGothicExpandedOne-Regular.ttf` via `@font-face` locally. Do not rely on Google Fonts for this.

### Color Direction (Derived from current portfolio aesthetic)
The current Framer site uses atmospheric 3D renders, moody accents, and a premium "eerie" quality. The new site should **evolve** this — more white space, more breathing room, but keep the atmospheric eeriness.

Suggested palette direction (confirm with user before finalizing):
- **Background:** Near-white `#F8F8F6` or pure white `#FFFFFF` — white space is intentional and premium
- **Foreground / Text:** Near-black `#111111` or `#0D0D0D`
- **Accent:** A single muted, eerie accent — could be a cool slate, deep green, or desaturated warm tone. Avoid bright/saturated colors.
- **Metric callouts:** The headline numbers should be massive, using Special Gothic Expanded One
- **Subtle texture:** Consider a very light grain or noise texture on backgrounds to reinforce the eerie, atmospheric quality

> **Do not finalize palette without confirming with Abdulmuiz. Surface options visually before committing.**

## 7. Aesthetic & Design Direction

### The Overall Vibe
**"Quiet confidence with an eerie edge."**  
Not loud. Not trying hard. The white space IS the statement. The eeriness comes from subtle unexpected moments — a cursor interaction, a parallax element slightly out of pace, a metric that appears one digit at a time on scroll.

### What to Reference
- **His current Framer site:** https://laja.framer.website — atmospheric 3D renders, floating objects in surreal natural settings (cubes in grass, glowing orbs, desert vignettes). Evolve this, don't replicate it.
- **Ethan Clark designer site:** https://ethan-clark-designer-16.aura.build/
- **Marcel Creative Portfolio:** https://marcel-creative-portfolio-1.aura.build/

### Layout Philosophy
**Anti-cookie-cutter.** Explicitly do NOT do:
- ❌ Hero image → "Selected Work" thumbnail grid → About Me section → Contact footer
- ❌ Project cards with title + category + arrow
- ❌ Generic "I design beautiful products" headline
- ❌ Skills listed as bullet points or tag clouds

**Do:**
- ✅ White space used as a design element, not absence of content
- ✅ Case studies that feel like editorial storytelling, not slide decks
- ✅ Metrics as typographic moments — large, confrontational numbers
- ✅ Navigation that is minimal and disappears when not needed
- ✅ Layouts that break the grid in deliberate, purposeful ways
- ✅ Strong typographic hierarchy — Special Gothic Expanded One doing heavy lifting on headings

### Animation & Interaction Principles
- **Subtle, not showy.** Animations should feel like breathing, not performing.
- Scroll-triggered text reveals (fade + slight upward drift)
- Metric counters that count up when they enter the viewport
- Cursor-reactive elements (subtle — perhaps a small trail or the cursor changes near interactive elements)
- Gentle parallax on atmospheric images
- No full-screen loading spinners or dramatic page transitions that feel like they're compensating

## 9. Technical Direction

### Recommended Stack
- **Next.js** (App Router) + **Tailwind CSS** — for routing, performance, and styling control
- OR **Pure HTML/CSS/JS** if the user wants zero framework complexity and to deploy to Netlify/Vercel easily
- **Confirm stack preference with Abdulmuiz before starting**

### Font Setup
```css
@font-face {
  font-family: 'Special Gothic Expanded One';
  src: url('/fonts/SpecialGothicExpandedOne-Regular.ttf') format('truetype');
  font-weight: 400;
  font-style: normal;
  font-display: swap;
}
```
Place the TTF file in `/public/fonts/` (Next.js) or `/fonts/` (plain HTML).

### SVG Logo Integration
The logo SVG (`La_ja_.svg`) is a pure path-based wordmark with `fill="white"`. Embed it directly inline in the HTML for full CSS control over color, or reference it as an `<img>` tag. For the dark-on-light usage, apply `filter: invert(1)` or provide a separate dark version by changing fill to `#111111`.

### Animations
Use **CSS transitions + Intersection Observer API** for scroll-triggered animations — no heavy GSAP unless complexity demands it. Keep the bundle lean.

For metric counters: vanilla JS counter function triggered by Intersection Observer.
